<h2>Overview</h2>



<p>

Wishly Store is an e-commerce project designed around the idea of making it easier for customers to discover and purchase gifts for different occasions.

</p>



<p>

The project focuses on providing a clean and simple online shopping experience.

</p>



<h2>Project Focus</h2>



<ul>

<li>Online product browsing</li>

<li>Gift-focused shopping experience</li>

<li>Product presentation</li>

<li>Customer-oriented interface</li>

<li>Simple and accessible shopping flow</li>

</ul>



<h2>Project Goals</h2>



<p>

The goal of Wishly Store is to provide a modern digital shopping experience for customers looking for gifts and products suitable for different occasions.
